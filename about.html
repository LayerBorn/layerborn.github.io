About

Small studio.
Big colors.

Layerborn is a small 3D printing studio based in Portland, Oregon. Every product is designed, sliced, printed, and shipped by real humans who actually care whether it comes out right.

We don't drop-ship and we don't resell generic models. Every design in the shop is original — sketched, modeled, prototyped, and refined until it looks good and works as intended.

Custom orders are welcome. Most take 1–2 weeks from "yes let's do it" to "delivered to your door" — sometimes faster, sometimes a little longer depending on complexity.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does shipping take?
Since every order is printed from scratch, expect about 1–2 weeks from order to doorstep. Rough breakdown:
  • Print time: 1–4 days depending on size and complexity
  • Packaging, label, and drop-off: 1–2 days
  • Standard US shipping in transit: 3–6 days
Larger or more elaborate orders can take a bit longer. If you need something by a specific date, message us first and we'll tell you honestly whether we can hit it.
Can you make my color combination?
Yes, in almost every case. We can blend multiple colors in a single print and keep a healthy stock of common ones. Use the contact form (or DM us before ordering) and tell us exactly what you want.
Can you print something from my own model?
Sometimes. Send us your STL or 3MF via the contact form and we'll take a look. If the model is print-ready and we have the materials, we'll quote you. We don't print firearms, copyrighted characters, or anything we don't feel right making.
Are these dishwasher safe?
No — 3D-printed plastics warp under dishwasher heat. Hand-wash with mild soap and warm (not hot) water. They'll last years if you treat them like nice ceramics.
What if it arrives damaged?
Message us as soon as you can after delivery (a photo helps) and we'll make it right — usually with a reprint. Stuff happens in transit; we've got you.

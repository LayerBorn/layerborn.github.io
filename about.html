About

Small studio.
Big colors.

Layerborn is a one-person 3D printing studio based in Portland, Oregon. Every product is designed, sliced, printed, and shipped from the same desk by a real human who actually cares whether it comes out right.

We don't drop-ship and we don't resell generic models. Every design in the shop is original — sketched, modeled, prototyped, and refined until it looks good and works as intended.

Custom orders are welcome and usually take 3–7 days from "yes let's do it" to "your package shipped."

FAQ

Good questions.

How long does shipping take?
Each item is printed after you order it, so factor in 1–3 days for the print itself plus standard USPS shipping (2–4 days for most US addresses). Total: usually within a week, often faster.
Can you make my color combination?
Yes, in almost every case. The AMS holds up to four colors per print, and we keep a healthy stock of common ones. Use the contact form (or DM us before ordering) and tell us exactly what you want.
Can you print something from my own model?
Sometimes. Send us your STL or 3MF via the contact form and we'll take a look. If the model is print-ready and we have the materials, we'll quote you. We don't print firearms, copyrighted characters, or anything we don't feel right making.
Are these dishwasher safe?
No — PLA and PETG both warp under high heat. Hand-wash with mild soap and warm (not hot) water. They'll last years if you treat them like nice ceramics.
What if it arrives damaged?
Message us within 48 hours of delivery and we'll reprint it for free. Stuff happens in transit; we've got you.
